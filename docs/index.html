Reading it. Points above the dashed line mean Yes happened more often than the price said. The slope b comes from logit(P(Yes)) = a + b·logit(price): above 1, prices are too timid at the extremes (favourites underpriced, long shots overpriced); below 1, too confident. Its standard error is clustered by event, because markets in one event move together. "Early closes" are markets that closed over a day before their scheduled end, usually because the event already happened; prices measured from the actual close then peek at the answer, which is why the default view measures 7 days before the scheduled end.
